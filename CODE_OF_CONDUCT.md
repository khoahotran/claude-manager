# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome different viewpoints, accept constructive criticism, focus on what is best for the
project, and do not harass or demean anyone. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing other people's private
information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules, and may ban repeat offenders.
Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers (GitHub private advisory, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or the
contact on the owner's profile). Reports are handled confidentially.
